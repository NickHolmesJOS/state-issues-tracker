Raw CSV columns
ColumnMeaningGrain / notes
BILLING_PROVIDER_NPI_NUM NPI of the provider that submitted the bill. May be blank; part of the duplicate/attribution key.
SERVICING_PROVIDER_NPI_NUM NPI of the provider that delivered the care. May be blank; used as a fallback provider id.
HCPCS_CODE Procedure / service code (HCPCS Level II or CPT). Valid = 5 chars (5 digits, or a letter + 4 digits).
CLAIM_FROM_MONTH Service month in YYYY-MM format. Feeds the monthly & yearly time bins.
TOTAL_PATIENTS Distinct patients in that provider × code × month roll-up. Already aggregated per row (not per patient).
TOTAL_CLAIM_LINES Number of claim lines in that roll-up. Denominator for per-line averages.
TOTAL_PAID Dollars paid for that roll-up. Core spend measure; summed everywhere.

Grain: each CSV row is one (billing NPI × servicing NPI × HCPCS × month) roll-up — not one claim and not one patient. This matters for how the metrics below are interpreted.

Metric definitions
Metric Aggregation How it is calculated
Total Paid Summed Sum of TOTAL_PAID over every sampled row.
Total Patients Summed Sum of TOTAL_PATIENTS. Not de-duplicated across months — a person seen in 3 months counts 3 times.
Claim Lines Summed Sum of TOTAL_CLAIM_LINES.
Avg Paid / Line Derived Total Paid ÷ Claim Lines.
Avg Paid / Patient Derived Total Paid ÷ Total Patients.
Unique Providers / HCPCS Deduped Distinct NPIs and distinct HCPCS codes seen in the sample.
Data Quality % Derived Share of rows with no major issue (missing NPI, duplicate, invalid code, or payment outlier).
Duplicate Claim Rows Deduped Rows repeating the key (billing NPI, servicing NPI, HCPCS, month). First is kept; each repeat counts as a duplicate.
Financial Exposure Summed Paid dollars sitting on rows with a quality issue, capped at Total Paid to avoid double-counting.
Spend Concentration Derived Providers ranked by summed paid; % of total held by the top 1% / 10% / 10.
Clinical Category totals Binned + Summed Each HCPCS binned into a clinical category (Level II by leading letter, CPT by numeric range), then paid/lines/claims summed per category.
Monthly & Year-over-Year Spend Binned + Summed Paid summed into month / year bins from CLAIM_FROM_MONTH; YoY % is the change from the prior year.
Costliest Codes (Avg/Line) Derived Per-code paid ÷ lines; codes with < 10 lines excluded as noise. ×Avg = code avg ÷ sample-wide avg.
Peer-Comparison Outliers Derived For each code, a provider's avg/line vs. the code-wide peer avg. Flagged at ≥ {{ a.peer_outlier_multiple if a.available else 5 }}× with ≥ {{ a.peer_min_lines if a.available else 20 }} lines and 3+ peers.
Optimization Potential Summed Duplicate-payment dollars + per-line outlier dollars + peer-benchmark excess (provider paid above peers × their lines).
