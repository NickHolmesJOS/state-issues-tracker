rows without major issues

Derived
Share of rows with no major issue (no duplicate, missing NPI, invalid code, or payment outlier). Higher is better — green ≥90%, amber ≥70%, red below.

Summed Total Paid, Total Patients and Claim Lines are the arithmetic sum of the TOTAL_PAID, TOTAL_PATIENTS and TOTAL_CLAIM_LINES columns across every sampled row. Derived the per-line and per-patient averages divide those sums, and Data Quality % is the share of rows with no major issue.
Caveat: each CSV row is a monthly (provider × code × month) roll-up, so Total Patients counts a patient once per month they appear — it is not a de-duplicated count of unique people.

Summed Financial Exposure sums the TOTAL_PAID dollars sitting on rows that tripped a data-quality rule (duplicates, per-line outliers, missing NPI, invalid code). Derived Spend Concentration ranks providers by their summed paid dollars, then reports what share of the total the top 1%, top 10% and top 10 providers represent.

Provider Peer-Comparison Outliers

For each procedure code we treat every billing provider as a peer group and compare each provider's average payment-per-line to the code-wide peer average. Providers billing ≥ {{ a.peer_outlier_multiple }}× the peer average (with at least {{ a.peer_min_lines }} lines and 3+ peers) are flagged — a classic signal for up-coding or overbilling on the same service others deliver far more cheaply.
